Privacy Policy

SAINT SOCIAL · St Andrews, Fife · Effective 15 September 2026

The short version

SAINT SOCIAL is a community app for students in St Andrews. We collect the minimum data needed to run accounts, we don't sell your data, we don't run ads or third-party trackers, and you can ask us to delete your account at any time.

1. Who we are

SAINT SOCIAL (this website and app) is run by the SAINT SOCIAL team for students and residents of St Andrews, Scotland. Questions about this policy: admin@stsocial.app.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data — if you create an account: your display name, email address, and any profile details you choose to add (course, year, bio, interests, avatar). Your password is stored only as a salted hash — we can never read it.
  • Content you create — posts, events, comments and replies you publish in the app.
  • Preferences — your theme (light/dark) and notification settings, stored in your browser/device only.

3. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your precise location. The app shows St Andrews content by default and the map/weather use fixed coordinates for the town.
  • We do not run advertising, analytics or tracking pixels of any kind.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone for marketing.

4. How your data is used

  • To sign you in and show your profile and posts.
  • To show community content to other users according to the privacy level you choose (public / students only / private).
  • To send the in-app notifications you have switched on.

5. Third-party services the app talks to

  • Open-Meteo — daily weather forecast (a fixed location query for St Andrews; no account or personal data).
  • Google Maps — the map view uses a standard embedded map; those requests go directly from your device to Google under Google's own policies.
  • Public information sources — event listings and news are pulled from publicly available university, society and venue calendars.

6. Data retention & your rights

Your data is stored for as long as your account exists. You can edit your profile anytime, and you can request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing admin@stsocial.app — we will act on verified deletion requests promptly.

7. Children

The app is aimed at university students and residents (typically 18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change and the new policy will be posted at this address before it takes effect.
